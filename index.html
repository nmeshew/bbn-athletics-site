Meet Coach Nick

Marine. Competitor. Coach.

Nick Meshew was born into an Army family at Fort Riley, Kansas, and grew up near Fort Knox, Kentucky. He served six years in the U.S. Marine Corps with 3rd Battalion, 2nd Marines, India Company, and is a combat veteran of the Gulf War.

After the Marines he spent more than 30 years in information technology, rising to Director of IT for an international law firm headquartered in London.

Nick has trained since he was 14. He has coached Division I athletes, retired NFL players, endurance athletes, mountaineers and competitive bodybuilders. At 56 he still competes: five NPC shows since 2024, and a medal at every one, most recently 2nd place in Masters Over 50 at the 2026 NPC Hurricane Bay.
